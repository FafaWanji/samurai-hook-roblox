A deadly serious samurai goes fishing, and no fish gets reeled in without a duel. Drop your hook, steer past the cheap fish, hook a good one and fight it one on one: nine fish from a sardine to a giant squid, and none of them wants to be sushi. Sell your catch, upgrade your blade, line and reputation, and go deeper.

Controls: A/D move, J quick slash, K heavy slash, L block (tap with a direction to dash), W jump, S duck, E shop, Tab pause. Gamepad and touch supported.
